## 💰 Cost Optimization Strategies

### **Corporate Discount Options**
1. **JBS USA Corporate Partnership:** Check if JBS has Coursera for Business
2. **University Alumni:** Use university email for potential discounts
3. **Professional Associations:** Check if you're member of engineering societies

### **Bundle Deals & Timing**
- **Black Friday/Cyber Monday:** Up to 50% off specializations
- **New Year Promotions:** January often has enrollment discounts
- **Summer Sales:** June-August academic year promotions

### **Payment Plans**
```
Monthly Option: $49/month (most flexible)
Annual Option: $399/year (save ~30% on multiple courses)
Individual Course: $39-79 (if you only want specific courses)
```
